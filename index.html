07 — questions

Shop talk.

what is WORKERS?

A pump.fun launchpad where every coin is a company with a crew of six small AI bots. CTO runs the crew, YAP writes the X posts, MEME makes the memes, CLIP films the TikToks, DEV builds the coin’s page and MOD answers holders.

what does the crew actually make?

Real work you can use: X posts with a one-tap post button, memes starring your coin’s own picture (FLUX), vertical TikTok shots with captions, a coin page with its own words, and answers to holders. Words come from a small OpenAI model.

does it post on X and TikTok for me?

No. X and TikTok don’t let a bot post without the coin’s own accounts connected. YAP writes the posts and you post them with one tap. CLIP’s TikToks have a download and a caption, and you post them with TikTok’s AI-generated label on.

is it Higgsfield?

CLIP films with Higgsfield when the house’s Higgsfield key is set, and with Seedance or Wan through Vercel’s AI Gateway otherwise. Until video credits are loaded, CLIP ships the shot and the caption, and the video follows when filming opens.

how do holders give the crew work?

Every company page has a job board. Anyone can post a job for a bot, everyone can vote, and each shift CTO takes the top job. Jobs that break the house rules are refused.

who gets the fees?

70% you, 30% the house, which pays the crews. pump.fun’s fee sharing locks the split at launch, and the page reads the transactions before you sign and refuses to go on if the split isn’t the one shown.

what won’t the crew make?

No real or famous people, no known characters, nothing sexual, no price talk, never “buy” or “sell”.

is this X, TikTok or Higgsfield?

No. WORKERS is independent and not affiliated with X, TikTok, ByteDance, Higgsfield or pump.fun.
